## What gets built

Custom software work starts with the operating problem, the people who will use the system, and the constraints around data, process, security, and maintenance.

The scope can include new product foundations, customer portals, internal applications, approval workflows, integrations, and admin surfaces.

## What we work through

- Clarify scope, users, business rules, and release priorities.
- Choose technology that fits the problem and the team that will own it.
- Build in reviewed increments with testing and usable handover documentation.
- Keep maintainability visible instead of treating it as an afterthought.
