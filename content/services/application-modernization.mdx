## The problem this service addresses

As applications age, they often become difficult and risky to change. Critical business logic becomes entangled across legacy frameworks, deployment processes remain slow and fragile, and key dependencies reach end-of-life status. When developers fear touching core modules due to unforeseen side effects, product roadmap velocity grinds to a halt.

Defaulting to a complete rewrite, however, carries tremendous commercial risk and typically takes far longer than anticipated.

## When application modernization is the right choice

- **Systemic change resistance:** Minor feature requests take weeks or months due to brittle codebase coupling and lack of automated tests.
- **Outdated tech stacks:** Frameworks, runtimes, or database versions require urgent upgrades to maintain security, compliance, and developer productivity.
- **Slow and fragile release cycles:** Deployments require manual steps, extensive weekend maintenance windows, or frequent rollback events.
- **Scalability bottlenecks:** Core database queries or background queues struggle under peak operational volume.

## What Archex delivers

We modernize systems safely and incrementally without halting ongoing business operations:

- **Architecture and dependency audit:** Mapping code dependencies, identifying security vulnerabilities, and pinpointing highest-friction bottlenecks.
- **Characterization test coverage:** Writing automated integration and end-to-end tests around existing critical paths to establish a protective safety net.
- **Incremental refactoring:** Decoupling monolithic components using the Strangler Fig pattern, exposing clean modern APIs, and modularizing business logic.
- **Safe data and infrastructure migration:** Migrating databases, cloud hosting providers, and message queues with zero-downtime cutover strategies and rollback paths.

## Engineering considerations and trade-offs

- **Preserving embedded business wisdom:** Legacy code contains years of resolved edge cases. We focus on understanding and testing these rules before refactoring.
- **Continuous production verification:** Modernization occurs alongside active product development, ensuring releases deliver immediate tangible value at every milestone.
- **Developer experience:** Modernizing local developer tooling, linting, and CI pipelines so internal teams can ship with speed and confidence.

## How the work progresses

Modernization engagements progress through Archex’s structured four-stage model: **Understand** (codebase audit and risk mapping), **Plan** (stabilization strategy and test boundaries), **Build and validate** (incremental refactoring and migration), and **Deliver and improve** (production deployment, monitoring, and handover).
