## Improve the system before replacing it

Existing systems often contain years of business rules. A rewrite may be right in some cases, but the safer path can be a sequence of stabilization, tests, refactoring, integration repair, and selective migration.

## Work we can assess

- Architecture and codebase assessment.
- Risk reduction through tests and release discipline.
- Database, platform, framework, or service migration.
- Long-term maintainability and operational improvement.
