## AI features with a way to check them

AI features need a job to do and a way to check the result. The right solution may use model APIs, retrieval, structured extraction, classification, summarization, or a simpler non-AI path.

## Questions the build must answer

- Define the task and acceptable failure modes.
- Set data boundaries and permission rules.
- Evaluate quality with representative examples.
- Add monitoring, escalation, and human oversight where needed.
