## Why incremental

Incremental migration lets a team improve high-value areas while keeping the business running. Depending on the system, the plan may include module extraction, database transition, API boundaries, or platform moves.

## Guardrails

- Start with the most painful or risky areas.
- Keep rollback and compatibility in view.
- Add tests before changing critical behavior.
- Avoid splitting systems in ways the team cannot operate.
