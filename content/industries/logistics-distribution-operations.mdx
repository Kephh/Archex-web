## Operating context

Logistics and distribution operations depend heavily on time-sensitive coordination among dispatchers, warehouse staff, drivers, and external freight carriers. Systems in this sector are often a mixture of modern cloud platforms and aging legacy on-premise databases, making unified visibility challenging to maintain.

Small discrepancies in shipment statuses or inventory counts can cascade into missed delivery windows, idle equipment, and costly customer SLA penalties.

## Recurring engineering challenges

- **Real-time status tracking and dispatch:** Providing operators with clear, responsive views of fleet allocations, route progress, and shipment states.
- **Exception handling workflows:** Automating alerts and re-routing protocols when delays, weather interruptions, or damaged shipments occur.
- **Legacy system integration:** Bridging older warehouse management systems (WMS) or EDI pipelines with modern web APIs and cloud event streams.
- **Offline and low-connectivity resilience:** Designing mobile or terminal interfaces for warehouse personnel that handle intermittent network drops without data loss.

We design operational dashboards, automated alert pipelines, and event-driven integration layers. We prioritize data accuracy, lightweight frontend rendering for ruggedized warehouse hardware, and clear audit logging for operational accountability.
