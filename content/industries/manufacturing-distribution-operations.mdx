## Operating context

Manufacturing and industrial distribution environments rely on tightly orchestrated handoffs between raw material procurement, assembly lines, quality assurance, and packaging. In many plants, critical work instructions, batch records, and defect reports still circulate on paper traveler sheets or disconnected spreadsheet macros.

Bringing software to industrial operations requires understanding physical facility constraints, touch screen ergonomics on the plant floor, and integrating with existing ERP or telemetry systems.

## Recurring engineering challenges

- **Shop floor work order tracking:** Digitizing work order progress, step completion, and operator sign-offs across multi-stage production runs.
- **Batch tracking and material traceability:** Maintaining an immutable record of raw material lot numbers, machine assignments, and quality inspection results for regulatory traceability.
- **Maintenance and downtime scheduling:** Recording preventative maintenance cycles and capturing real-time machine downtime codes to inform scheduling decisions.
- **Shop-floor interface design:** Creating high-contrast, touch-optimized user interfaces that operators can navigate quickly while wearing protective gear.
