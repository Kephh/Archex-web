## Common problems

Operations-heavy organizations often live with spreadsheets, email chains, manual approvals, and aging internal systems that no longer match how work is actually done.

## Engineering approaches

Relevant work includes custom workflow tools, integrations with existing systems, reporting improvements, role-based access, audit trails, and gradual replacement of fragile manual processes.
