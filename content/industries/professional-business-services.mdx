## Operating context

Professional service firms—including legal, consulting, accounting, advisory, and agency practices—routinely exchange sensitive client documentation, track multi-stage approvals, and coordinate project deliverables. When these processes run via sprawling email threads and shared spreadsheets, work is delayed, visibility is lost, and operational overhead compounds.

Developing software for professional services requires striking a balance between rigorous confidentiality, intuitive client collaboration, and streamlined internal handoffs.

## Recurring engineering challenges

- **Client intake and document ingestion:** Capturing structured project requirements, onboarding documentation, and client assets through secure, self-guided portals.
- **Multi-stakeholder approval pipelines:** Managing conditional approvals, signatures, and document versioning with comprehensive audit trails.
- **Role-based permissions:** Restricting document access and project visibility strictly to authorized client and internal team members.
- **Reporting and utilization visibility:** Aggregating status, milestone completion, and billing records without tedious end-of-month manual reconciliation.

## Practical engineering approach

Archex engineers purpose-built client portals, secure document management workflows, and internal coordination tools. Systems are designed around clear data models, role-based access control (RBAC), and reliable automated notifications that keep projects moving predictably.
