## Operating context

Software and SaaS companies face constant pressure to ship user-facing capabilities while maintaining the reliability, performance, and security of existing services. As a product matures, technical debt, evolving data structures, and third-party dependencies can slow release momentum.

When exploring AI features, product engineering teams must avoid treating models as black boxes that compromise system trust or introduce unpredictable failure modes for users.

## Recurring engineering challenges

- **Feature velocity versus foundation stability:** Adding new product capabilities while keeping test suites fast, deployments predictable, and regressions rare.
- **AI feature integration:** Implementing retrieval-augmented flows, structured extraction, or classification with clear boundaries, confidence scoring, and fallback states.
- **Tenant isolation and permissions:** Ensuring multi-tenant data safety, granular permission boundaries, and auditability as customer tiering expands.
- **API and integration health:** Designing resilient external webhooks, partner APIs, and background job processing that degrade gracefully under load.
