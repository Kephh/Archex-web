## The existing system knows things

Legacy software often looks messy because it has absorbed years of business exceptions. Replacing it without understanding those exceptions can move risk rather than remove it.

## A more informed sequence

Start with assessment, stabilize critical flows, make behavior observable, add tests around important rules, and migrate where the value justifies the disruption.
