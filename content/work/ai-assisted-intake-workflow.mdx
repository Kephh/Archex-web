## Context

This illustrative pattern shows how a team might improve document-heavy intake. It describes an engineering approach, not a customer or measured outcome.

## Challenge

The workflow involves repetitive review, incomplete submissions, internal routing, and the need to preserve human accountability.

## Approach

The build would combine structured extraction, review queues, confidence thresholds, audit logs, and evaluation sets based on representative examples.

## What this example demonstrates

This is an illustrative workflow pattern, not a client case study. The important design choices are traceability, reviewable decisions, and measured model behavior without removing people from accountable steps.
